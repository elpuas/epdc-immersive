# Creative brief

An immersive digital world that helps a first-time visitor understand why to hire ElPuas: relevance to their needs, evidence through real projects, and people behind delivery.

Combine the favorite cinematic floating panels with simple Tron-inspired flat terrain and a perspective grid. Avoid complex rock textures and physical reflections. Use black #000000, lime #D8FF00, cyan #00FFF3, dark olive #5E6F00 and readable neutral text. The preferred latest reference uses colored accents; a gray-wireframe alternative is also retained for comparison. Exact intensity remains open.

A prompt illuminates a route and moves the camera to a destination. Start with authored options, not simulated claims of live AI. Keep navigation available. Prioritize calm movement, strong composition, readable panels, mobile and reduced motion.

No watercolor, painted website, ribbon or branching scroll narrative in the current concept.
